This week focused on moving the Disaster Response and Emergency Verification project from prototype into a more complete emergency reporting workflow. The work touched public pages, dispatcher tools, admin alert handling, mobile tracking, backend routes, and hosting behavior.

The biggest improvement was report tracking. Users could look up reports by ID, while dispatchers gained clearer claim and handoff visibility. On the backend, route handling and API validation became safer for Vercel deployment.

## What I Practiced

- Building dispatcher workflows for claiming, passing, and tracking reports.
- Connecting realtime alert updates with admin actions.
- Improving public pages without breaking report submission and tracking flows.
- Fixing hosting, database, and media-storage issues across MongoDB, Supabase, and Vercel.

## Outcome

SVS-MDRRMO became more reliable, easier to use, and closer to a complete emergency reporting platform for citizens, dispatchers, and admins.
