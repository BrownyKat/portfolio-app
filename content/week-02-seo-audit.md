This week centered on technical SEO. I reviewed metadata, page headings, crawl paths, image usage, semantic HTML, and performance signals. The work was less about chasing a single score and more about creating a repeatable audit process.

I documented each finding with severity, affected page area, and a recommended fix. That format made the audit easier to act on because the next step was always visible.

## What I Practiced

- Reading Lighthouse output without treating it as the whole truth.
- Writing recommendations that engineering and content teams can both use.
- Prioritizing fixes by user and business impact.

## Outcome

The audit became a reusable checklist for future internship pages and portfolio updates.
