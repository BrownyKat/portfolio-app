This week centered on DeskDine, a lunch ordering system for admin, staff, and customer users. The work moved from product pitch into implementation, testing, bug fixing, and presentation.

I worked on checkout and order handling, receipt generation, post-order features, menu management, staff management, payment proof handling, revenue tracking, and mobile interface cleanup. The project also involved repeated bug hunting and UI/UX refinement across both web and mobile views.

## What I Practiced

- Turning an operational friction point into a working product concept.
- Designing admin, staff, and customer flows around real ordering tasks.
- Debugging database write/update behavior and receipt-related issues.
- Simplifying payment handling into a general e-wallet proof-of-payment flow.
- Cleaning redundant mobile code so the app is easier to modify.

## Outcome

DeskDine reached a presentable state with core ordering, receipt, revenue, and management workflows in place, then was checked by Sir Carl before being added to the portfolio.
